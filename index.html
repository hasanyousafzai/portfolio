01 / About

Backend that scales.
AI that can be trusted.

I'm a Senior Software Engineer with 6+ years building and operating production systems. My centre of gravity is Java & Spring Boot — microservices, RESTful and event-driven APIs, and secure cloud-native platforms serving government and commercial clients. I currently lead the Data Management service area at SPS across DB2, MySQL, and SQL Server.

Over the last stretch my work has moved deeper into applied AI. I built a production RAG pipeline that classifies incoming documents with Watsonx OCR/LLMs and Milvus vector search against records-management schedules — a problem where retrieval quality, not model choice, decides whether the system can be trusted. That meant owning chunking strategy, embedding choices, and evaluation, not just wiring an API call.

I also ship Flutter and native Android apps — offline-first, clean architecture, real-device integrations — with 13 platforms and apps delivered across Google Play and the App Store. Being fluent in both planes means I design APIs that are actually pleasant to consume, because I'm usually the one consuming them.
